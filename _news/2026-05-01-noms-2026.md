Our paper ["Kernel Bypass Surgery: A Viable Procedure for Maximizing QUIC Bandwidth?"](https://zirngibl.github.io/files/spaeth2026quicbypass.pdf) was accepted at NOMS'26.

We investigate the impact of kernel bypass on performance.
We integrated the Data Plane Development Kit (DPDK) into three QUIC implementations, bypassing their use of the kernel networking stack, and updated a fourth stack that previous work combined with DPDK.
Our analysis shows that kernel bypass can greatly increase the goodput achieved with QUIC, with a speedup reaching up to a factor of 3× and goodput over 10 GBit/s.
However, the reachable performance increase highly depends on the implementation, and in one case performance did also not further increase when using DPDK.
All [implementations and artifacts](https://github.com/tumi8/quic-bypass-paper) are available.
