Starting in 2026, I am a co-lecturer for [Data Networks](https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/departments/inet/teaching) at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics and the Saarland University.
The lecture takes a dive into how data networks work, and how they work together to create the network of networks we know as the Internet.
Previously, I was head teaching assistant for the course.
